# Plain writing capability

## Purpose

Turn technical evidence into direct prose without losing qualifications, precision, or useful terminology.

## Entry conditions

Use during final synthesis after facts, inferences, and uncertainties have been separated.

## Method

1. Lead with the outcome or finding.
2. Use concrete nouns and verbs and remove empty intensifiers.
3. Preserve necessary technical terms, citations, and uncertainty labels.
4. Remove repetition that does not change the reader's decision.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the result can be read once without hiding nuance or stripping necessary detail.

## Evidence

The concise final synthesis is part of the owning root run; stylistic edits never replace substantive evidence.

The shared output contract applies this same internal pass to all twelve QS core commands, eight QS specialist commands, and thirteen PS commands. It never becomes a nested invocation or a separately reported skill.

## Owners

The PS catalog owners remain `ps-help`, `ps-how`, `ps-why`, `ps-blast-radius`, `ps-runtime-forensics`, `ps-trace-forensics`, `ps-create-verification-skill`, `ps-maintain-verification-skill`, `ps-skill-eval`, `ps-hillclimb`, `ps-visual-parity`, `ps-pr-babysit`, and `ps-worktree-cleanup`; QS ownership is enforced by the shared output contract rather than by importing the PS skill body.
